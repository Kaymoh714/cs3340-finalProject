About this Business

I developed this webpage because I enjoy playing Warhammer 40,000 - a tabletop game where you and a friend battle different armies, but the part of the game where you need to assemble and paint your own miniatures became too daunting to me, especially since I am not a good painter.

I ended up developing this website and listing the more complex miniatures available in the game that people play, in the hopes that they would want to reach out to have their models professionally painted. In the rules of the game you only need to have a basic paint on your characters, though for some of the more notable units people tend to decorate them even further as display pieces. This is why I offered both painting styles as options, so the consumer would have the choice as to how detailed and coloured they want their miniatures.

Services like this already exist through indpendent contractor sites, like Fiverr or Etsy, however there are not that many centralized websites dedicated to painting Warhammer 40,000 miniatures. By creating an e-commerce site for that specific purpose, I found I can list the units that I would like to try painting, while also allowing for users to set their standards and also submit their own recommendations for units to list on the site.
